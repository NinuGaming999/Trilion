---
name: minecraft-building
description: Place blocks and build simple structures in Minecraft precisely.
---
1. Select the block with mc_hotbar. Blocks are placed on the FACE of an existing block: mc_aim at the face where the new block should attach, then mc_use.
2. Build one row at a time. After every few placements, look at the screenshot and compare it with the plan; fix mistakes by mining the wrong block with mc_mine.
3. For walls: stand 2-3 blocks back, build the bottom row, then jump (mc_walk with jump) or stack up. Hold sneak (mc_walk with sneak) near edges so you do not fall.
4. In Creative mode you can fly: double-tap space (game_act tap space twice quickly) and use space / shift to go up and down.
